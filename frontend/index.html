How settling works
  1. Top-league games open automatically, a few days ahead.
  2. Stakes close at kickoff.
  3. After full time, validators read the result from the match feed and must agree on the 90-minute score.
  4. If the feed is silent for 6 hours, they can read a match page with AI instead. Then collect your winnings.
