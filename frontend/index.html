Back the result. Validators read the final whistle.

Every fixture opens four markets: the result, over or under 2.5 goals, both teams to score, and an open correct-score pool. Stakes go into shared pots and winners split the pot of their market.

  1. Someone lists a match with a link to its match page. Validators check the page really shows that fixture before it opens.
  2. Stakes are accepted until kickoff.
  3. Two hours after kickoff, anyone can ask validators to settle. Each validator reads the match pages on its own and they must agree on the 90-minute score.
  4. All four markets settle from that score. Postponed or abandoned matches are refunded.
